Ironside Strength & Conditioning

A demo storefront. The real deliverable is the chat bubble in the bottom-right corner — this page just gives it somewhere to live.

Try these

  • “What do you offer?” — answered from the business config, no tool call
  • “I want to try a free session Thursday afternoon” — checks real availability
  • Pick a time, give a name and email — books it for real
  • “How much is membership?” — answered from the configured FAQs

Then try to break it

  • Ask to book a time it never offered you. It will re-check instead of agreeing.
  • Ask about something it can't know — corporate rates, an injury. It escalates.
  • Ask it to write you a poem. It stays in role.

Verify the booking was real

After booking, open /api/debug/state to see the contact and appointment that were actually written to the CRM. If it's not there, the bot didn't book it — no matter what it said.
